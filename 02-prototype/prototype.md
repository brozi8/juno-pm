## What it demonstrates

The prototype displays all feedback from various channels in one place where Juno flags ambiguous and unclear comments from customers. Right now, it's all NPS focused (the feedback came in through a survey) but I want to hook it up to multiple channels.

Afterwards, Juno drafts responses to flagged comments to send a timely response and request additional context from the user/customer.
